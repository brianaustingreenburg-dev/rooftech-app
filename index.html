MY STORY

Jack of all trades, master of nothing — until now.

LA Studios started as a dream. Years ago I was fishing the cold waters of Alaska, pulling long shifts on boats most people only see on TV. If you've watched Deadliest Catch, you know what that life looks like — months at sea, little sleep, dangerous conditions every single day. That's where I learned that it's not about how many layers you put on. It's about the quality of each layer.

Safety first. Stay healthy. Make your money and go home.

Now in my 50s, living in Acapulco, Mexico, I challenged myself again. Long nights in front of a computer, brainstorming, building, putting ideas into practice. RoofTech is the result — built by someone who's actually worked the trades, not a committee in a San Francisco office.

I know some of you love fishing as much as I do. So I set up an entire travel package through the app — fishing trips, resorts, the whole thing. Whether you want to work or unwind, this app works for you.

Stay up & blessed,
